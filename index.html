CanyonGlow Lead Intake & Approval Automation

An n8n workflow that captures fictional HVAC service enquiries, triages them with deterministic rules, uses an LLM only for language tasks, and keeps every customer message under human approval.

Fictional demo. All data is made up. Results come from controlled test runs, not real customers. The public enquiry form is not included in this repo.

How it works

  1. Receive: a form posts to an n8n webhook, which creates a submission ID.
  2. Validate: input is trimmed and normalized; required fields, formats and consent are checked. Unknown fields are rejected.
  3. Deduplicate: recent records are checked. Duplicates are flagged and kept, never deleted.
  4. Apply rules: service area and priority (high, medium, low) are set deterministically.
  5. Summarize: an LLM returns a short summary, missing information, confidence and a review reason as strict JSON. It cannot override the rules.
  6. Record: the lead is written to a Google Sheets register, with an audit log entry.
  7. Approve: a reviewer gets an email with one-time approve and reject links.
  8. Log: the approval workflow checks the token, updates the lead, logs the decision without personal data, and invalidates the link.

Design principles

Rules decide, AI assists

Priority and service area never depend on the model.

Human in the loop

High-priority, uncertain, outside-area and pricing messages need approval.

Safe fallback

Invalid AI output or confidence below 0.75 routes the lead to manual review.

One-time links

An approval link works once. Reusing it returns “Link not found or expired”.
